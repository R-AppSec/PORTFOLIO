About

I'm a Senior Application Security Engineer with 11 years of hands-on experience securing enterprise applications, mobile platforms, APIs, and connected vehicle ecosystems.

Over the past 5+ years at Toyota Motor North America, I've led security testing and validation efforts across the connected vehicle platforms, identifying and validating 446+ security findings across critical infrastructure protecting 13+ million vehicles. My work directly contributes to automotive security compliance frameworks including SAE J3161, SOTIF, GDPR, and CCPA.

I'm passionate about discovering real-world vulnerabilities, understanding attack vectors, and building secure systems from the ground up. I love the intersection of security engineering and automotive technology, where security decisions directly impact millions of users.

Currently interested in: Connected vehicle security, API security hardening, threat modeling, automotive compliance frameworks, and mentoring next-generation security engineers.
